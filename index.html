FUNGALWAREA SMALL PLAYABLE EXPERIMENT

01—03 / A CANOPY, A LITTLE RAIN

Rain Choir.

Tilt the mushroom caps. Guide each drop to a thirsty root.
Make the whole garden bloom before the rain runs out.

Preparing the game. A small download, then everything runs here.

One drop at a time.

Click a cap to tilt it, or press A, S, D. Press Space to release rain. You can tilt a cap while a drop is still sliding.

Every root wants a drink.

A full root wastes extra water. The first two scenes let you take your time. In the third, Space starts a short rainstorm. 1–3 selects a scene.

Take a breath.

P pauses. R restarts. H shows help. Leaving the tab pauses the garden; resume when you return. Best played with a keyboard or mouse.
